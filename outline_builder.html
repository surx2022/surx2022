Put the speech back together.

Pick an organizational pattern. You’ll get 19 paragraphs from one speech, shuffled, including the transitions between main points. Place each paragraph where it belongs in the outline, one section at a time.

Attention grabberRaise your hand if…
Main point 2What happened during…
Memorable closingThe next time you…

To place a paragraph, drag it into the outline, or tap the paragraph and then tap its spot. Hover over or tap the ? next to any outline prompt to see what it means. When a section is full, it’s checked automatically.

Textbook reference

Hazlett, A. D., Jangbar, S., Harrison, M. A., Bahrainwala, L., & Ford, J. L. (2023). Organizing a successful presentation. In N. R. Repay, M. A. Harrison, & J. A. Daly (Eds.), Introduction to professional communication.
